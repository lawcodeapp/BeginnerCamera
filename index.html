FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Beginner Cam free?

Yes. Every subject comes with free guides and looks. You can also try every Pro guide and look for free; one payment unlocks saving and sharing those photos. There is no subscription.

Do I need any photography experience?

No. Beginner Cam was made for people who are just starting. Pick what you are shooting, line your subject up with the guide, and press the shutter.

Does Beginner Cam upload my photos?

No. There is no account and no upload. Your photos stay on your iPhone, and the guide lines are never saved into them.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later.

Is there an Android version?

Beginner Cam is on iPhone today. An Android version is planned.
