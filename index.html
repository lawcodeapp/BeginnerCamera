FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Beginner Cam free?

Yes. Every subject comes with free guides and looks, and photo and video are free. You can try every Pro guide, look and Dual for free; one payment unlocks saving and sharing those shots and removes the small Beginner Cam mark from your photos. There is no subscription.

Can I film video?

Yes. Switch to Video and film with the same guides and looks, then trim, crop, change the look or mute the clip. Dual films with the back and front cameras at the same time, so you get the stage and your own face in one video or photo.

Do I need any photography experience?

No. Beginner Cam was made for people who are just starting. Pick what you are shooting, line your subject up with the guide, and press the shutter.

Does Beginner Cam upload my photos?

No. There is no account and no upload. Your photos and videos stay on your phone, and the guide lines are never saved into them.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or later, through Google Play, and iPhones running iOS 16 or later once the App Store version is out.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Beginner Cam is available for Android on Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.
