Composition cheat sheet: 30 guides

Every composition guide in Beginner Cam on one page, each with its lines and a one-line tip, from easiest to hardest. "Subject" boxes show where the main thing goes; dashed boxes are space to keep empty. Print it or save it for your next shoot.

Easy (10)

  • Rule of thirdsPut the subject on a crossing.
  • Center focusPlace the subject in the center.
  • Low horizonKeep the horizon low. Show sky.
  • High horizonKeep the horizon high. Show ground.
  • Top-downHold the phone flat above.
  • Mirror symmetryLine up the center line.
  • Eye lineKeep the eyes on the top line.
  • Head and shouldersFit the head and shoulders in the outline.
  • Fill the frameGet close. Fill the whole frame.
  • ColumnKeep tall things straight in the middle.

Medium (14)

  • Leading linesLet lines lead to the subject.
  • Frame in frameFrame the subject with a doorway.
  • Negative spaceLeave lots of empty space.
  • LayersShow near, middle and far.
  • Lead roomLeave space where they look.
  • Golden ratioLike thirds, a little closer to the center.
  • Flat layLay things in a neat grid. Shoot from above.
  • Side lightLet the light come from one side.
  • PairPut two subjects on opposite lines.
  • TriangleArrange three points in a triangle.
  • RadialLet things spread out from the center.
  • Half splitSplit the frame into two halves.
  • Vanishing pointAim where the lines meet.
  • S-curveFollow a gentle S into the scene.

Advanced (6)

  • Diagonal flowFollow the diagonal across the frame.
  • L-frameArrange things along an L.
  • Rule of oddsGroup things in threes.
  • Cross diagonalsPlace things along both diagonals.
  • Break the patternFind a pattern, then break it.
  • Golden spiralLet the curve wind to the subject.
