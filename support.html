Purchases

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Your purchase is tied to the Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Sign in to the same account on your new phone, open Beginner Cam and tap "Restore Purchases". This also works after deleting and reinstalling the app. A purchase made on iPhone does not carry over to Android, or the other way around.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment that unlocks all Pro guides and looks for good. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Where did my photo go? It wasn't saved.

Photos made with a Pro guide or look are kept in the app's Held tray until you unlock Pro, so they are never lost. Once you unlock Pro, you can save or share them from there.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and choose the purchase. On Android, request one from your Google Play order history or see Google Play's refund policy. If you get stuck, email us and we'll help.

Camera and saving

The camera shows a black screen or asks for access

Beginner Cam needs camera access to show the live preview. On iPhone, open Settings > Beginner Cam and turn on Camera. On Android, open Settings > Apps > Beginner Cam > Permissions and allow Camera. Then reopen the app.

Photos aren't saving to my library

If the photo used a Pro guide or look, it is waiting in the Held tray. Otherwise, on iPhone open Settings > Beginner Cam > Photos and choose "Add Photos Only". On Android, check that your phone has free storage space.

Will the guide lines show up in my photo?

No. Guides are shown on screen only while you shoot and are never saved into your photos.

How do I turn off reminders?

Turn them off in the app's Settings. You can also use iPhone Settings > Beginner Cam > Notifications, or Android Settings > Apps > Beginner Cam > Notifications.

Privacy and data

Do you upload or see my photos?

No. There is no account and no upload. Your photos stay on your phone unless you share them yourself. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app stores about you lives on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. There is no account to delete. The only copy kept elsewhere is your anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat, our purchase service. To have it deleted, email us with your App Store or Google Play order ID and we'll do it within 30 days. This does not cancel or refund your purchase.

Devices

Which phones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later. On Android, get Beginner Cam on Google Play; the store page shows whether your phone is supported.
