Purchases

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID. Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Beginner Cam and tap "Restore Purchases". This also works after deleting and reinstalling the app.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment that unlocks all Pro guides and looks for good. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Where did my photo go? It wasn't saved.

Photos made with a Pro guide or look are kept in the app's Held tray until you unlock Pro, so they are never lost. Once you unlock Pro, you can save or share them from there.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and choose the purchase.

Camera and saving

The camera shows a black screen or asks for access

Beginner Cam needs camera access to show the live preview. Open iOS Settings > Beginner Cam and turn on Camera, then reopen the app.

Photos aren't saving to my library

Open iOS Settings > Beginner Cam > Photos and choose "Add Photos Only". If the photo used a Pro guide or look, it is waiting in the Held tray instead.

Will the guide lines show up in my photo?

No. Guides are shown on screen only while you shoot and are never saved into your photos.

How do I turn off reminders?

Turn them off in the app's Settings, or in iOS Settings > Beginner Cam > Notifications.

Privacy and data

Do you upload or see my photos?

No. There is no account and no upload. Your photos stay on your iPhone unless you share them yourself. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app stores lives on your iPhone, so deleting the app removes it. There is no account or server copy to delete.

Devices

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later.

Is there an Android version?

Beginner Cam is on iPhone today. An Android version is planned.
